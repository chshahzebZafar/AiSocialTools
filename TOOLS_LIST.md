# Social Media Tools - Complete List

## Recommended Tools (Top 8)

These are the tools marked as "Recommended" in the application - perfect for getting started:

### 1. **Buffer** ⭐ 4.5/5
- **Category**: Scheduling & Management
- **Pricing**: Free plan available, paid plans from $6/month
- **Best For**: Small to medium businesses, content creators
- **Key Features**: Post scheduling, analytics, team collaboration, content calendar

### 2. **Hootsuite** ⭐ 4.4/5
- **Category**: Scheduling & Management
- **Pricing**: Free plan available, paid plans from $99/month
- **Best For**: Enterprise teams, agencies
- **Key Features**: Multi-platform scheduling, social listening, advanced analytics

### 3. **Canva** ⭐ 4.8/5
- **Category**: Design & Graphics
- **Pricing**: Free plan available, paid plans from $12.99/month
- **Best For**: Non-designers, small businesses
- **Key Features**: Templates, drag-and-drop editor, brand kit, photo/video editing

### 4. **Later** ⭐ 4.6/5
- **Category**: Scheduling & Management
- **Pricing**: Free plan available, paid plans from $25/month
- **Best For**: Instagram-focused creators and businesses
- **Key Features**: Visual content calendar, Instagram scheduling, link in bio tool

### 5. **Linktree** ⭐ 4.5/5
- **Category**: Link Management
- **Pricing**: Free plan available, paid plans from $6/month
- **Best For**: All social media users
- **Key Features**: Multiple links in bio, customizable themes, analytics

### 6. **Grammarly** ⭐ 4.7/5
- **Category**: Content Creation
- **Pricing**: Free plan available, paid plans from $12/month
- **Best For**: Content creators, writers
- **Key Features**: Grammar checking, tone detection, plagiarism checker

### 7. **CapCut** ⭐ 4.6/5
- **Category**: Video Editing
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Best For**: Video content creators
- **Key Features**: Video editing, templates, text animations, auto-captions

### 8. **Unsplash** ⭐ 4.8/5
- **Category**: Stock Media
- **Pricing**: Free
- **Best For**: All content creators
- **Key Features**: High-resolution photos, free to use, curated collections

## Getting Started Recommendations

**For Beginners:**
1. Start with **Canva** for design
2. Use **Buffer** or **Later** for scheduling
3. Add **Linktree** for link management
4. Use **Unsplash** for free images

**For Small Businesses:**
1. **Hootsuite** or **Buffer** for management
2. **Canva** for design
3. **Grammarly** for content quality
4. **CapCut** for video content

**For Agencies/Enterprise:**
1. **Sprout Social** or **Hootsuite** for advanced management
2. **Brandwatch** for social listening
3. **BuzzSumo** for content research
4. **Socialbakers** for AI-powered insights
