## ✍️ Content Writing Guidelines

### Tone & Voice
- **Friendly but Professional**: Approachable expert
- **Clear & Concise**: Easy to understand
- **Action-Oriented**: Encourages tool usage
- **Value-Focused**: Emphasizes benefits
- **Trustworthy**: Builds credibility

### Writing Principles
1. **User-First**: Always write for the user's benefit
2. **SEO-Optimized**: Natural keyword integration
3. **Scannable**: Use headers, bullets, short paragraphs
4. **Actionable**: Include clear CTAs
5. **Engaging**: Tell stories, use examples

### Content Length Guidelines
- **Blog Posts**: 1,500-3,000 words (comprehensive)
- **Tool Descriptions**: 200-400 words
- **Meta Descriptions**: 150-160 characters
- **Headlines**: 50-60 characters
- **Social Posts**: Platform-appropriate
